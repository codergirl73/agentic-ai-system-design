# Interview Preparation

Interview preparation is organized module by module.

## Main question bank
See [MODULE_WISE_QUESTIONS.md](MODULE_WISE_QUESTIONS.md) for beginner, intermediate, and advanced questions across all nine curriculum modules.

## Per-topic standard
For every topic, include:
- 3–5 basic questions
- 3–5 intermediate questions
- 2–3 advanced system-design questions where relevant
- Explained answers and reasoning
- Follow-up questions and trade-offs
- A short mock interview exercise

Practice explaining assumptions, alternatives, failure modes, evaluation, and why a design choice is appropriate.

Company-style prompts are maintained separately in [SYSTEM_DESIGN_INTERVIEWS.md](../SYSTEM_DESIGN_INTERVIEWS.md).
