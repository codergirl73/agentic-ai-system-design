# Research Papers

## Module 1 — Foundations of Agentic AI

### ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models
- Authors: Shunyu Yao et al.
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629
- Focus: interleaved reasoning and action; agent execution loop.

### Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools
- Authors: Timo Schick et al.
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761
- Focus: tool selection and language-model tool use.

### A Survey on Large Language Model Based Autonomous Agents
- Authors: Lei Wang et al.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-024-40231-1
- Focus: agent architecture, planning, memory, tools, and evaluation.
