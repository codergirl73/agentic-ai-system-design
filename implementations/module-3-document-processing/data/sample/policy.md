# University Assignment Policy

## Submission deadlines

Assignments are due by 11:59 PM on the date shown in the course portal. Students should confirm that the portal displays a successful submission receipt.

## Late submissions

Late work loses 10 percent per calendar day unless an extension has been approved. The deduction is applied to the assignment score, not to the course grade directly.

## Approved extensions

Students with an approved extension should use the revised deadline recorded by the instructor. The revised deadline replaces the original deadline for that assignment.

## Questions and support

For questions about submission status, contact the course teaching team and include the course code and assignment name. Do not include passwords or authentication tokens in support messages.
